**1. Snapshot**
Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) is an Indian manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors. In Q1 FY27, the company reported consolidated revenue of ₹1,248 crore (up 18% YoY) and a profit after tax of ₹82 crore (up 5.9% YoY). It holds a strong market position in power transmission and data-centre supply, backed by a robust ₹3,900 crore order book.

**2. Bull Case**
*   **Capacity Expansion:** The new ₹620-crore Bharuch (Gujarat) plant is 70% complete and on track for commercial production in Q3 FY27, which will expand total cable capacity by 30%.
*   **Improving Governance Profile:** Promoter share pledging has reduced drastically from 35% in March 2024 to 4.1% in June 2026, while combined FII and DII holdings rose to 26.4%.
*   **Export and Niche Growth:** Exports increased to 21% of revenue (up from 16% YoY). High-margin data-centre cables now contribute 9% of domestic revenue, with management projecting this share to double in two years.

**3. Bear Case**
*   **Margin Compression:** EBITDA margins contracted by 170 bps YoY to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, driven by a 14% surge in raw copper costs.
*   **Stretched Working Capital:** Receivable days rose from 78 to 96 days over the past year, primarily due to payment delays from two large state utility customers.
*   **Tax Litigation:** The company faces a pending GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalties) for FY21 and FY22, which it is currently appealing.

**4. Open Questions**
*   **Revenue Discrepancy:** There is a major conflict regarding Q1 FY27 revenue; the official company press release (Tier 1) reports ₹1,248 crore (+18% YoY), whereas Business Daily (Tier 2) reports ₹1,428 crore (+35% YoY).
*   **Margin Recovery Timeline:** With a 1-to-2 quarter lag in resetting price-variation clauses, how long will elevated copper prices continue to suppress profitability?
*   **Receivable Resolution:** What is the expected timeline for recovering the outstanding dues from the slow-paying state utilities?
